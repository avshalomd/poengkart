# Official Udir/vigo vocabulary only, Norwegian canonical

Labels invented for the app ("category", "Alle med poeng", "fortrinnskvote", "round") read more clearly to us than the sector's own words, and the temptation to coin them recurs with every new screen. We decided (August 2026, recorded 2 September 2026) that every user-facing term, and over time every identifier in code and docs, uses the wording families already meet on vigo.no, vilbli.no and in the counties' own tables, with Udir's Grep hierarchy (utdanningsprogram → programområde → the county's intake grouping) as the structure. Norwegian is canonical; English is a gloss. `CONTEXT.md` is the glossary and the place a new term is settled. Reason: families, rådgivere and county officials must recognise the app as describing the system they know; a clearer invented label costs that recognition and cannot be undone once it has spread through screenshots and outreach. Model-internal terms with no official Norwegian equivalent (forecast, fill probability, backtest) stay English and never reach a family raw.
